# Layer 4: Model Layer – Listed US and Hong Kong/China AI Model and Platform Companies

## Overview

Layer 4 in Jensen Huang’s stack is the “model layer,” where foundation models and specialized AI systems learn the languages of text, code, images, biology, physics, and more. Economically, this layer is dominated by large cloud providers and internet platforms integrating foundation models into their products, alongside a growing set of pure‑play enterprise AI platforms and newly listed Chinese model labs in Hong Kong.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

This report maps listed US and Hong Kong/China names with direct economic exposure to training, hosting, and monetizing large models. It focuses on:

- US hyperscalers and platform companies commercializing foundation models.
- US-listed enterprise AI software and model-ops platforms.
- Hong Kong/China internet giants with in‑house foundation models.
- Newly listed Chinese foundation-model companies on HKEX.

## US Hyperscalers and Model Platforms

In the US, Microsoft, Alphabet (Google), Amazon, and Meta are the primary listed companies that either own leading frontier models or aggregate access through cloud platforms. They monetize models via cloud APIs, SaaS copilots, ads, and enterprise integrations, often partnering with private labs like OpenAI and Anthropic while also building their own models.[^2][^6][^1]

## Important Non‑Listed but Strategically Central Labs

Several of the most influential model labs remain private or are subsidiaries within public companies:

- OpenAI – foundation models (GPT family) powering Microsoft Copilot and many third‑party applications; private, with Microsoft holding significant economic rights.[^1][^2]
- Anthropic – Claude model family, distributed via Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, and direct APIs; Amazon and Google hold equity stakes.[^7][^2][^1]
- DeepSeek – Chinese open‑weight model lab noted for DeepSeek‑R1 and V4, which deliver near frontier-level performance at low cost and catalyzed significant market reactions.[^25][^26][^27][^15]
- Moonshot AI, Baichuan, and others – Chinese “AI tigers” with strong open‑model output and deep backing from BAT and other tech investors.[^22][^28][^15]

Public investors gain indirect exposure via the hyperscalers and Chinese internet giants that invest in or distribute these labs’ models.

## How to Use This Layer‑4 Company Map

The tables above provide a starting investment universe for the model layer, spanning US and Hong Kong/China listings. A practical approach could:

- Use US hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, IBM, PLTR) as core exposure to both proprietary and aggregated foundation models.
- Add US enterprise AI platforms (C3.ai, ServiceNow, Salesforce, Adobe, Snowflake) for leveraged exposure to model deployment and orchestration in specific verticals.
- Allocate to Hong Kong/China internet giants (Baidu, Alibaba, Tencent, SenseTime) as the primary vehicles for China’s domestic model layer, recognizing regulatory and sanctions risk.
- Consider new HKEX model specialists (MiniMax, Zhipu AI) as higher-beta, earlier‑stage plays on Chinese foundational models, with careful attention to liquidity, governance, and export‑control dynamics.

Given rapid technical progress and regulatory flux, Layer 4 investing requires continuous monitoring of model benchmarks, pricing, and ecosystem partnerships, alongside standard financial and governance analysis.[^24][^2][^15][^1]
